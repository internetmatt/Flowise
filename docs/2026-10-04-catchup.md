## Ownership and next concrete slice

-   IdeaFlow: graph-to-skill binding/export, native payload preservation and the optional graph UI.
-   Host/harness: authorized dispatch, durable runs, artifacts, browser attach and approval enforcement.
-   Routine/metadata adapters: calendar occurrence identity, vault/Drive bindings, OpenIdea import/export, idempotency and conflict handling. Confirm the extracted repository before opening implementation PRs there.
-   Content adapters: storyboard and deck/ad rendering; inspect OfficeCLI before declaring its capabilities.

First acceptance target: one synthetic OpenIdea record imports, binds to one pinned executable skill, runs through a verified authorized adapter, and exports its updated status plus durable artifact references. Prove that result before adding calendar dispatch, Drive delivery or multi-output content generation. Use the same run for the optional UI; preserve each system's native graph semantics.

These ADRs are review material for work at ideus, not claims of adopted employer architecture. No employer repository or external message is changed by this PR.
